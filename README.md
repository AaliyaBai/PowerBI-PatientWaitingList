# PowerBI-PatientWaitingList
## Project: Patient Waiting List Management
### Overview
This Power BI project focuses on the management and visualization of patient waiting lists within a healthcare facility. It aims to provide an efficient way to track patient appointment wait times, waiting list volume, and identify bottlenecks in service delivery.
Dataset Description
The dataset used for this project consists of Inpatient details and Outpatient details stored in two different datasets. Below are the key fields captured in the ### ### Dataset:
#### •	Case_Type: The category of the medical case (e.g., Day Case, Inpatient or Outpatient).
#### •	Adult_Child: Indicates whether the patient is an adult or a child.
#### • Speciality_Name: The medical specialty the patient is waiting for (e.g., Cardiology, Dermatology etc).
#### •	Age_Profile: The age group of the patient 
#### •	Time_Band: Time intervals indicating how long a patient has been on the waiting list.
#### •	Total: The total count of patients for the specific category.
 
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of patient waiting lists across various medical specialties. It highlights average and median wait times for departments such as Orthopaedics, ENT, Dermatology and Paediatric Urology. The data is broken down by Age Profile (0-15, 65+) and Time Bands (e.g., 0-3 months, 18+ months), giving insights into how long patients are waiting for care. The dashboard also tracks wait times by Case Type (Outpatient, Day Case, Inpatient) and trends in patient volumes over time from 2018 to 2021.
